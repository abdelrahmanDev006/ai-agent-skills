# Template: Versus & Comparison (Chaos vs Order)

Direct contrast is the highest-converting video format for B2B and Creator tools. This template establishes a brutal, clear visual distinction between the painful old way and the modern automated way.

---

## 1. Visual Dichotomy Design System

| Dimension | The "Old Way" (Chaos / Competitor) | The "Modern Way" (Your Platform) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Color Theme** | Subdued slate `#64748B`, error red `#EF4444`, alert amber `#F59E0B` | Vibrant brand blue `#2563EB`, emerald green `#10B981`, crisp white `#FFFFFF` |
| **Layout** | Tangled badges, crooked borders, noisy clutter, manual checkmarks | Asymmetric bento grid, perfect alignment, rounded cards (`rounded-2xl`), live counters |
| **Pacing** | Chaotic, stuttering, hesitant pauses | Confident, smooth spring physics (`damping: 14`), rapid confirmations |
| **Status Pills** | ❌ إيصال مزوّر • روابط مسربة • 30% عمولة مستقطعة | ✓ تشفير عالي • 0% عمولة • تحصيل فوري ومباشر |

---

## 2. Timing Breakdown (15s Duration)

1. **Frames 0 – 110 (0.0s – 3.7s): The Wake-Up Hook**
   - Direct question attacking the status quo: *"ليه بيع كورساتك بالنظام القديم بيضيّع 50% من مجهودك؟"*
   - High-contrast badge with warning icon.

2. **Frames 110 – 230 (3.7s – 7.7s): Side-by-Side Comparison**
   - Dual-card split:
     - Left card (Old Way): Grey background, red cross indicators, messy typography.
     - Right card (New Way): Clean white background, glowing emerald checkmarks, organized hierarchy.

3. **Frames 230 – 345 (7.7s – 11.5s): Tangible Proof & Value Transformation**
   - Big numerical payoff: Time saved (+15 hours/week), profits preserved (+30,000 EGP saved from platform commissions), or automated enrollments.

4. **Frames 345 – 450 (11.5s – 15.0s): Unified Outro**
   - Brand logo, verified official URL, and low-friction guarantee row.
